How the grid works

Companies join the grid

Nearby companies opt into a shared commute zone instead of sourcing drivers on their own.

Drivers get jointly verified

Background checks and verification are shared across every company on the grid, not repeated by each one alone.

Employees pick a zone and ride

Employees choose the pickup zone closest to them and book a seat on a verified route.

For employees

  • Ride with a driver verified through the grid, not an unknown app driver
  • Fixed pickup zones and times — no address shared with drivers or other riders
  • Shared with coworkers from other grid companies on the same route

For companies

  • Share verification and liability instead of vetting drivers alone
  • Lower cost per employee by pooling routes with neighboring companies
  • Fewer vehicles arriving at your office park each day

Verification is shared, not assumed

Every driver on the grid is verified once, by the joint venture of participating companies — not re-checked, or skipped, by each company separately.
